## Grand Tour adaptation · 11 October 2026

The trip app adds fifty unlockable stages across ten fictional circuits inspired by its Japan itinerary. Procedural Japan scenery replaces Palm Cove in the active game, with five route/light variants per destination. The original environment implementation remains in the source for attribution/reference.

The garage adds Kitsu and playful friend nicknames, animated 3D costume variations, photographic face decals and cosmetic-only coin unlocks. Photographic assets are not included in this repository: authorized organizers upload them privately through the existing Momenti service.

Shared progress and leaderboards require the updated Momenti Google Apps Script deployment. Scores are private, authenticated, validated and deduplicated, but this is a friendly leaderboard rather than a tamper-proof competitive service. Original game and Three.js MIT licenses remain included.
